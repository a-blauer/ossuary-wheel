# Ossuary Wheel

A phone-controller party game: a dark fantasy dungeon crawl where every fight is a mini golf course on the monster's back.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages site on a big screen (a computer or a TV browser) and choose **Host**. Everyone else scans the QR code with their phone and joins. No accounts, no install.

- Phones connect straight to the big screen (WebRTC, through the free PeerJS service). Phones on the same Wi-Fi as the big screen connect most reliably.
- `lab.html` is the mini-game lab: pick any trap or event floor and play it on its own.
